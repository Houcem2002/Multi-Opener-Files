3. Data Deletion & Export

Users have full autonomy over their data. Through our in-app Privilege & Privacy Center, users can instantly clear all local caches, databases, preferences, or completely revoke their targeted ad consent with immediate effect. We retain no user data on our own servers following app deletion.
