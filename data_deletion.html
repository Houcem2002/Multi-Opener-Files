Data Deletion Policy & Instructions

Effective Date: June 2026

1. Data Retention

Multi Opener Files is designed to process files locally on your device. We do not store your files on external servers. However, some temporary data, caches, or preferences may be stored securely on your device.

2. How to Delete Your Data

You have full control over your data. To request and execute the deletion of all your local application data and preferences, please follow these instructions:

  1. Open the Multi Opener Files application.
  2. Navigate to the Settings screen using the gear icon.
  3. Select the Privacy Center card.
  4. Tap on Delete Data under the "Data & Consent" section.
  5. All local application data, caches, and preferences will be permanently wiped from your device.

3. Advertising Identifiers

If you have consented to personalized advertising, an advertising identifier may be associated with your device via Google AdMob to serve relevant ads. You can manage, revoke, or delete this consent at any time via the Privacy Center -> Ad Consent setting in the app, or by resetting your device's advertising ID in your Android Settings.
